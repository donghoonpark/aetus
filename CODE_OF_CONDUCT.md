# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want AETUS to be a practical, respectful engineering space. Contributors and users are expected to treat each other with patience, clarity, and good faith.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially across different experience levels.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on tradeoffs, evidence, and project goals.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for firmware, backend, data, and frontend contributors alike.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private credentials, private telemetry, or vulnerability details without coordination.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after maintainers have asked to refocus.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; restrict participation; or ban contributors when necessary to protect the project and community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer.
